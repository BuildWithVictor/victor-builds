The concept

Healthy food without the clinical personality.

ERA explores a stronger consumer direction for meal subscriptions: appetite-first imagery, expressive typography and a warm orange-and-green system instead of the usual sterile fitness aesthetic.

The demo includes menu filters, weekly menu switching, a five-meal box builder, macro calculations, feedback interactions and carefully adapted responsive layouts.
