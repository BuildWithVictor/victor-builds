The product

Built around participation, identity and fandom.

Anime Social combines familiar social behaviours with anime-specific identity. The current build includes account authentication, profiles, feeds, discussions, blogs, polls, Hot Takes, comments and replies, reactions, reposts, saves, follows and notifications.

It also includes verification, anime associations, personal-library and progress foundations, moderation workflows and a separately authenticated Admin Control Centre.

Authentication · Profiles · Feed · Posts · Replies · Reactions · Reposts · Saves · Follows · Notifications · Polls · Hot Takes · Search · Verification · Library · Moderation ·

Under the interface

Server-side rules remain the source of truth.

Social actions, ownership checks, permissions, CSRF protection, prepared database operations and separate administration are enforced beyond the visible UI. The structure is designed so important business logic can be reused as the product develops.
