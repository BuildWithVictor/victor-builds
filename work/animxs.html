ANIMXS

An anime-first social platform built as a real PHP/MySQL product for discovering, discussing and connecting around anime and manga.

Type
Social web application
Role
Product design & full-stack development
Stack
HTML · CSS · JavaScript · PHP · MySQL

The product

One social space built around anime fandom.

ANIMXS brings social interaction and anime discovery into one product. The current build includes authentication, profiles, feeds, posts, communities, direct messaging, notifications, search and anime/manga discovery.

The platform also includes email OTP, moderation workflows, account controls, responsive mobile support and a dedicated Admin Control Centre for managing the growing product.

Authentication · Profiles · Feed · Posts · Communities · Messages · Notifications · Search · Anime Discovery · Manga Discovery · Reactions · Follows · Email OTP · Moderation · Admin Control Centre ·

Under the interface

A growing full-stack product, not a static concept.

ANIMXS combines a PHP/MySQL backend with server-side permissions, database-driven social features, API-powered catalogue discovery and administration tools. The product is being developed toward a public beta and real-user testing.
