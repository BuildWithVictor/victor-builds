REDESIGN GUIDE

When should a business redesign its website?

A redesign can help when the current site no longer represents the quality of the business.

The website looks outdated

Customers may judge a business quickly. A cleaner design, current information and professional images can build confidence.

It is difficult to use on a phone

Most customers browse on mobile devices. Important information and contact buttons should be easy to find on a small screen.

Pages load slowly or navigation is confusing

Compressed images, clearer menus and simpler layouts help visitors reach the information they need.

What the redesign includes

Victor Builds provides a fresh design, improved mobile appearance, clearer navigation, faster images and pages, and migration of approved existing content.
