WEBSITE COST GUIDE

How much does a business website cost in Nigeria?

The answer depends on the number of pages, features and whether your team needs an admin panel.

Starter website: from ₦50,000

A focused one-page website can suit a new business, campaign or personal brand. It should still work well on phones and include a direct way to enquire.

Business website: from ₦100,000

A multi-page website normally includes Home, About, Services and Contact pages, plus a contact form and basic Google setup.

Website with an admin panel: from ₦180,000

An admin panel allows your team to update approved content without editing code. More advanced systems are quoted according to their features.

Other possible costs

Domain registration, hosting, extra pages, special functions, professional copywriting and ongoing management may be priced separately.
