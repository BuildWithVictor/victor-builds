Project terms

Clear expectations before work begins.

01

Payments

A 50% deposit begins the project. The balance is due after approval and before launch unless agreed otherwise in writing.

02

Revisions

Each agreed scope includes a stated number of reasonable revision rounds. New pages, systems or major direction changes are quoted separately.

03

Ownership

Final approved website files are handed over after full payment. Domain and hosting accounts should remain under the client’s control.

04

Adult review

Contracts, payment terms and customer agreements must be reviewed by a parent, guardian or trusted adult business representative.
